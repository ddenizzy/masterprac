      How MasterPrac works

      1. Play: follows whatever plays on your Mac in any app (Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music...). It pulls the song's tab from Songsterr and scrolls it with the music. Jam shows the chord now and the notes that sound good over it; Learn the part shows the exact string and fret the guitarist plays. No tab for a song? The chords are worked out by ear.
      2. Songsterr tab: a browser built into the app. Open any song; MasterPrac recognises it and the other tabs follow it.
      3. When it opens, it finds the song Spotify is playing, mutes Spotify (still playing, so your minutes count; paused on battery) and moves the Mac's sound to your HeadRush over Bluetooth.
      4. When you close it, the sound goes back to your Kali speakers and Spotify's volume comes back.
      5. Your saved rigs are never changed.
