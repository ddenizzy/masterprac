      How MasterPrac works

      1. Now playing: follows whatever plays on your Mac in any app (Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music...). It finds the song on Songsterr and shows the chord now, the next chord, every part's chords, the notes to improvise with and where they are on the neck. No tab for it? It works the chords out by ear.
      2. Live notes: "Play along with the lead guitar" shows the exact string and fret the song's guitarist plays, in time with Spotify.
      3. Songsterr tab: a browser built into the app. Open any song; MasterPrac recognises it and the other tabs follow it.
      4. When it opens, it finds the song Spotify is playing, mutes Spotify (still playing, so your minutes count; paused on battery) and moves the Mac's sound to your HeadRush over Bluetooth.
      5. When you close it, the sound goes back to your Kali speakers and Spotify's volume comes back.
      6. Your saved rigs are never changed.
