MasterPrac

Learn keys and improv on the songs you love, with your HeadRush.

How MasterPrac works

  1. Left window = Songsterr. Open any song there; MasterPrac notices which song it is.
  2. This panel shows the song, where your sound goes, your amp and (soon) the fretboard with the notes you can play.
  3. When it opened, MasterPrac found the song Spotify was playing, muted Spotify (or paused it on battery), and moved the Mac's sound to your HeadRush over Bluetooth. The list at the bottom says exactly what it did.
  4. When you close it, the sound goes back to your Kali speakers and Spotify's volume comes back.
  5. Your saved rigs are never changed. Tone ideas go into one practice rig until you choose to save them.
